Who should apply

Candidate Eligibility

01 Currently enrolled in a relevant degree programme, or a recent graduate (within 12 months).
02 Basic knowledge of the chosen track — you don't need to be expert, but you need to have started.
03 A genuine 3–6 month commitment. Internships are structured programmes, not work experience days.

How to apply

Application Process

01

Review open tracks

Read through the eight internship tracks above and identify where your skills and interests align. There's no limit on which track you apply for — apply for the one that fits, not the one that sounds safest.

02

Submit your application & portfolio

Complete the application form below. For design tracks, include portfolio work. For technical tracks, include a GitHub link or relevant project examples. A cover letter is optional but genuinely read.

03

Assessment

A short practical task relevant to your chosen track. Not designed to be a test of perfection — designed to see how you approach a problem and communicate your thinking.

04

Interview

A one-hour conversation with the team lead for your track. We talk about your work, your goals, and what you want to get out of the programme. This is also your chance to ask hard questions about us.

05

Offer & onboarding

Successful candidates receive a formal offer letter with start date, team placement, and programme structure within five working days of the final interview.

What you get

Internship Benefits

✔

Paid internship. Every track carries a stipend — the work is real, so the compensation is too.

✔

Dedicated mentorship. Each intern is assigned a named mentor from the core team — someone responsible for your growth, not just your task list.

✔

Professional development. Access to training materials, internal workshops, and a review process that gives you structured feedback.

✔

Network. Work alongside people from multiple countries and disciplines — a rare experience at an early career stage.

✔

Potential full-time offer. Strong interns are offered positions. This isn't a formality — several current team members started as interns.
