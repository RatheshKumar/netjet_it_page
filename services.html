Services & Solutions

Build. Run.
Grow. Specialize.

Four clear disciplines. One integrated team. Everything from a first-release website to a multi-continent IT infrastructure — designed and delivered in Kochi, deployed everywhere.

01 / Build

Build & Develop

Whether it's a consumer app, an enterprise web platform, or a physical product brought to market, our Build practice turns ideas and requirements into shipped, working software and designed experiences. We own the brief from concept through to deployment.

Web Development

Full-stack web projects built to perform under load. E-commerce, corporate platforms, portals, and web applications developed with clean architecture and maintainable code.

Mobile App Development

Native and cross-platform mobile applications for iOS and Android. Built with Flutter, React Native, and platform-specific tooling — optimized for speed, usability, and app-store guidelines.

Custom Software Development

Bespoke software built around your workflow rather than the other way around. From internal tooling to client-facing platforms, we design and develop software that fits what you actually do.

Design — Branding, Print & Digital Creative

Visual identity, print materials, signage, and advertising creative. A dedicated design practice — NETJET Design Services — working across digital and physical formats.

New Product Design & Development

From early-stage concept validation and UX prototyping through to technical specification and first-release development — we help take new products from idea to market-ready.

02 / Run

Run & Manage

Once things are built, they need to run without interruption. Our Run practice covers the full stack of IT operations — cloud, network, security, data, and project governance — so your team can focus on the work that matters.

Cloud Services

Private, public, hybrid, and multicloud architectures across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud. IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS deployments — designed for scalability, compliance, and cost control.

Network Installation

Structured cabling, switching, wireless, and WAN design for offices and facilities. Commissioned, documented, and handed over with ongoing support agreements.

Server Maintenance

Proactive monitoring, patching, capacity planning, and incident response for on-premise and cloud-hosted server environments.

Cyber Security

Cybersecurity as a Service (CSaaS), SIEM deployment, endpoint protection, and continuous monitoring. Security posture assessment and compliance alignment for regulated industries.

Data Management & Security

Data architecture, backup strategy, encryption, access control, and regulatory compliance (GDPR, HIPAA-aligned). Making your data an asset, not a liability.

Repair & IT Support Services

Hardware repair, software troubleshooting, and managed helpdesk support for organizations that need reliable day-to-day IT without a full internal team.

Project Management

Dedicated project governance — planning, risk management, stakeholder communication, and delivery oversight — applied across both our own engagements and client-side programs.

03 / Grow

Grow & Market

Technology alone doesn't drive growth — the right message reaching the right audience at the right time does. Our Grow practice combines marketing execution with purpose-built marketing software and CRM capability.

Digital Marketing

Search engine optimization, paid social and PPC, email campaigns, content strategy, and community management — managed as a single, measurable effort rather than siloed channels.

Custom Marketing Software

When off-the-shelf marketing tools don't fit, we build them. Campaign management, lead scoring, analytics dashboards, and automation tools designed for your specific go-to-market model.

CRM Software

CRM consulting, system selection, custom development, data migration, user training, and ongoing support. Whether you're choosing a CRM for the first time or modernizing a legacy system, we manage the full journey.

eCommerce & Web Portal Development

Commerce platforms, B2B portals, and membership-gated web applications — built for conversion, integrated with payment gateways, and structured for long-term content management.

04 / Specialize

Specialized Services

Some challenges require more than a standard service catalogue. Our Specialize practice covers consulting, emerging technology, enterprise software, and applied research — for organizations ready to move beyond off-the-shelf solutions.

IT Consulting

Strategic planning, technology selection, system integration, security and compliance, performance optimization, and training — structured as advisory engagements rather than hours-based billing.

Artificial Intelligence

Automation & Robotization

Business process automation, workflow orchestration, and hardware-integrated robotics for manufacturing and logistics environments.

Big Data Services

Internet of Things

Custom ERP Software

HR Software

eLearning Software
